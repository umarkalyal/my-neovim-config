# tokyonight.nvim (colorscheme)

**Intro:** TokyoNight Night theme - clean, dark colorscheme with good contrast. Loaded with high priority.

## Usage
- Applied automatically on startup via `vim.cmd.colorscheme("tokyonight-night")` in `lua/plugins/colorscheme.lua`.
- To try a different TokyoNight variant, you can change `"tokyonight-night"` to `storm`, `moon`, or `day` in that file.

## Tips
- Works well with lualine/bufferline (both configured to use tokyonight theme).
